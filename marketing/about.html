About Hutchins Health Insurance

Hutchins Health Insurance is a family-owned agency dedicated to helping individuals, families, and seniors navigate the complexities of health insurance. With decades of experience serving clients across Kentucky, Tennessee, Georgia, Louisiana, and Florida, we provide personalized guidance tailored to your unique needs and circumstances.

Our mission is to make health insurance simple, accessible, and affordable. We take the time to understand your situation—whether you're seeking Medicare coverage, ACA marketplace options, supplemental insurance, or dental and vision plans—and we connect you with the best solutions available.

We believe that quality health insurance shouldn't be confusing or stressful. Our team of knowledgeable professionals is here to answer your questions, explain your options, and ensure you're making informed decisions about your healthcare coverage.
